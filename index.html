Frequently asked questions

What is SellerCalc?

SellerCalc is a free website with fee, profit and shipping calculators and guides for US online sellers on Amazon, Etsy, eBay, Walmart, Shopify, Whatnot, Poshmark and Mercari.

Are SellerCalc calculators free?

Yes, all SellerCalc calculators are free to use, with no sign-up and no download.

Do I need to sign up to use a calculator?

No, you do not need to sign up. Open a calculator, enter your numbers, and get an instant result.

Are the results exact?

No. Results are estimates and general information only, based on the numbers you enter and the fee rates shown on the page. Always check the platform's current fee page before you price or buy.
