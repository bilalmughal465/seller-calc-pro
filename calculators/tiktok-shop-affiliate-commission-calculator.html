TikTok Shop Affiliate Commission Calculator

Work out exactly what you pay creators for driving TikTok Shop sales, before you agree a commission rate.




—

Affiliate note: the affiliate commission rate is set by you, not by TikTok Shop, so this calculator takes it as your input. Published 2026 benchmarks describe typical open-collaboration rates around 10% to 20%, but your rate is whatever you set in Seller Center.

Formula

Affiliate commission per sale = affiliate rate × item price. Total affiliate cost = commission per sale × number of creator-driven sales. The TikTok Shop referral fee is a separate charge on top.

How it works

Enter the item price, the commission rate you set for creators and how many sales creators drove. The calculator shows the creator cost per sale and the total creator cost, so you can compare it against the referral fee and your product margin before raising a rate to attract more creators.

Worked example

A $28 item at a 15% affiliate rate, with 50 creator-driven sales: commission per sale 15% of $28.00 = $4.20. Total creator cost = $4.20 × 50 = $210.00. The TikTok Shop referral fee on those same sales is charged separately.

FAQs

Who sets the TikTok Shop affiliate commission rate?

You do, per product, in Seller Center affiliate tools. Reported ranges run from 1% to 80% in principle, with published 2026 benchmarks putting typical open-collaboration rates around 10% to 20% and top-selling-product medians near 9%.

Is affiliate commission paid on every TikTok Shop sale?

No. It is paid only on sales attributed to a creator through the affiliate program. Sales from your own videos or shop page without a creator link pay no affiliate commission.

Is the affiliate commission on top of the referral fee?

Yes. The referral fee goes to TikTok Shop and the affiliate commission goes to the creator. On an affiliate-driven order you pay both, which is why the combined take is much higher than the referral fee alone.

What is open collaboration versus targeted collaboration?

Open collaboration lets any eligible creator promote your product at your public rate. Targeted collaboration is an invite-only deal with a specific creator, usually at a higher negotiated rate.

Do creators still get paid if the buyer returns the item?

Commission on a returned order is clawed back, so the creator does not keep commission on a sale that did not stick. Use the creator payout calculator to model returns.
