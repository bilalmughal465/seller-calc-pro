Scientific calculator questions

What is DEG / RAD?

DEG means degrees and RAD means radians. Trigonometric functions (sin, cos, tan) use the mode shown on the DEG button. Tap the button to switch modes.

Which functions are included?

Sin, cos, tan, log (base 10), ln, powers (x squared and x to the power y), square root, pi, parentheses, plus, minus, multiply and divide.

Are the results exact?

No. Results are estimates and general information only, rounded for display. Always check important calculations yourself.
