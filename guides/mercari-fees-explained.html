Mercari Fees Explained

Short answer: Mercari charges sellers a flat 10% selling fee on the item price plus any buyer-paid shipping. Listing is free, and there is no separate seller payment processing fee on current listings.

Stand: October 2026. Source: Mercari Help — Fees on Mercari. The 10% structure applies to listings created or updated on or after January 6, 2025.

The fee in one line

Selling fee — 10% of (item price + buyer-paid shipping). No listing fee, no monthly fee, and no separate seller payment processing fee on current listings.

Buyers separately pay a 3.6% Buyer Protection fee on the same base (item price + buyer-paid shipping). That buyer fee is not a seller deduction, but it does raise what the buyer pays at checkout.

Worked example

A $40 item with $6.00 buyer-paid shipping. Fee base = $40.00 + $6.00 = $46.00.

Selling fee: 10% x $46.00 = $4.60. Amount before your own costs = $46.00 - $4.60 = $41.40. If you instead paid for the shipping label yourself, that label cost would come out of the $41.40 as well, so free shipping costs you twice over: once in the label, and once in the fee only if the buyer paid it — here the buyer paid it, so the fee already includes it.

For comparison, the same $40 item with no buyer-paid shipping pays 10% x $40.00 = $4.00, keeping $36.00 before costs.

Formula

Selling fee = 10% x (item price + buyer-paid shipping). Payout before your costs = (item price + buyer-paid shipping) - selling fee - any label cost you chose to pay yourself.

How it works

Mercari's fee is the same in every category, which makes pricing simple — but the shipping trap still applies. Charging the buyer for shipping raises the fee base, while offering free shipping leaves the fee on the item price alone and moves the label cost onto you. Compare both versions before you choose, and do not rely on old articles that call Mercari fee-free: that structure ended in January 2025.

Frequently asked questions

What is Mercari's selling fee?

Mercari charges a flat 10% selling fee on the item price plus any buyer-paid shipping, for listings created or updated on or after January 6, 2025. Listing is free.

Is there a separate payment processing fee for sellers?

No, not on current listings. Mercari states that no separate payment processing fee applies to listings created or updated on or after January 6, 2025.

What does the buyer pay on top?

The buyer pays a 3.6% Buyer Protection fee on the item price plus buyer-paid shipping. That fee is charged to the buyer, not deducted from the seller's payout as a seller fee.

Is the 10% fee charged on shipping I pay for?

The fee is charged on the item price plus buyer-paid shipping. If you offer free shipping and pay the label yourself, the fee applies to the item price, and the label cost comes out of your payout separately.

Does Mercari charge to list an item?

No. Listing an item is free on Mercari, whatever the category, price or volume.
