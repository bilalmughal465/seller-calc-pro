Poshmark vs Depop Fees: Which Keeps You More?

Short answer: On fees alone, Depop wins at every price — and not narrowly. A US seller pays only 3.3% + $0.45 in payment processing on Depop, while Poshmark takes a flat $2.95 under $15 and 20% at $15 or more. On a $100 sale Depop takes $3.75 and Poshmark takes $20.00, a gap of $16.25. But fees are not the whole cost: on Depop you fund the shipping label yourself, and on a $30 jacket with a $5.00 label that flips the result — Poshmark banks $24.00 and Depop banks $23.56. The table below shows both sides, and the worked example shows exactly where the flip happens.

Stand: November 2026. Sources: Poshmark Support fee and shipping pages ($2.95 under $15, 20% at $15 or more; $6.49 flat buyer shipping for a USPS Ground Advantage label up to 5 lb) and Depop Help Centre seller fees (0% selling fee for US sellers, 3.3% + $0.45 payment processing on item price plus buyer-paid shipping and tax; Boosted Listings 12%) — re-checked 21 November 2026. Two lines in that table do most of the work. First, Depop's 0% is a selling fee of zero — processing still applies, and it is charged on the item price plus buyer-paid shipping and tax, so charging the buyer for shipping raises your fee base. Second, Poshmark's 20% is all-inclusive: it covers payment processing, and the buyer pays the $6.49 label separately. The worked example: a $30 jacket, once you pay for shipping

The fee table above leaves shipping out. Put it back in and the result changes. Take a $30 jacket. On Depop you fund the label yourself — assume a $5.00 label, a typical small-parcel cost. On Poshmark the buyer pays the $6.49 flat label and you pay nothing for postage.

Check the maths. Depop, no Boost: $30.00 − $1.44 − $5.00 = $23.56. Depop, boosted: $30.00 − $1.44 − $3.60 − $5.00 = $19.96. Poshmark: $30.00 − $6.00 = $24.00, and the buyer pays $30.00 + $6.49 = $36.49 in total. The $4.56 fee gap from the table above is real — and the $5.00 label eats all of it, plus $0.44 more. The flip is worse on cheap items: a $12 top with a $5.00 label banks $12.00 − $0.85 − $5.00 = $6.15 on Depop, against $12.00 − $2.95 = $9.05 on Poshmark — Poshmark ahead by $2.90. And a boosted $30 sale on Depop banks $19.96, $4.04 behind Poshmark's $24.00, because the 12% Boost on top of processing is close to 16% all-in before the label. When Poshmark still wins — and when it does not

Poshmark wins when the label matters more than the fee. Light items under $15, heavy items near the 5 lb line, and anything where your Depop label costs $5.00 or more — the buyer-paid $6.49 label is worth more than the 20% costs you. Bundles are the same story: three $15 tops in one Poshmark parcel cost the buyer one $6.49 label, and you keep $36.00. Poshmark also wins on audience. Its buyers skew toward mainstream and premium brands, it has bundles and live selling built in, and an item that sells on Poshmark and sits unsold on Depop earns more on Poshmark, whatever the fee table says.

Depop wins when the fee gap is bigger than the label. Higher-priced fashion, streetwear, vintage and Y2K pieces where the buyer base is already on Depop — a $100 sale keeps $16.25 more on fees, and no label under $16.00 closes that. Depop also wins when you ship cheap: a light item in a poly mailer with a $4.00 label keeps most of the fee gap. Depop loses its edge when you Boost. A 12% Boost on a $30 sale costs $3.60 — nearly the whole fee gap — so Boost only items that are already priced and photographed well, and price for the 12% before you switch it on. Method and limits

The figures are 2026 US rates: Poshmark's published US rates ($2.95 flat fee under $15, 20% at $15 or more, $6.49 flat buyer shipping for a USPS Ground Advantage label up to 5 lb) and Depop's published US rules (0% selling fee for US sellers, 3.3% + $0.45 payment processing on item price plus buyer-paid shipping and applicable tax, Boosted Listings 12% of the item sale price on attributed sales), re-checked 21 November 2026. The $5.00 Depop label in the worked example is a stated assumption for a small parcel, not a published rate — your label cost depends on weight, size and zone, so run your own numbers before you list. Buyer-paid amounts such as Depop's buyer Marketplace fee are not deducted from the seller's keep figures. Refunds reverse the fees tied to the sale. All figures are US domestic, for sellers without any platform promotion. Fee rules change — check each platform's current fee page before you price. This is general information, not tax advice.

Frequently asked questions

Which has lower fees, Depop or Poshmark?

Depop, on fees alone, at every price. US sellers pay only payment processing on Depop — 3.3% + $0.45 — while Poshmark charges a flat $2.95 under $15 and 20% at $15 or more. On a $100 sale Depop takes $3.75 and Poshmark takes $20.00, a gap of $16.25. But fees are not the whole cost: on Depop you fund the shipping label yourself, and that can close or reverse the gap.

Is there a price where Poshmark becomes cheaper than Depop?

On fees alone, no — Depop's 3.3% + $0.45 is below Poshmark's 20% at every price, and below the $2.95 flat fee under $15. Once shipping is funded by the seller, yes: a $30 jacket with a $5.00 label banks $23.56 on Depop against $24.00 on Poshmark, where the buyer pays the $6.49 label. On a $12 top with a $5.00 label, Depop banks $6.15 against Poshmark's $9.05.

Does Depop really charge 0% fees?

Depop charges a 0% selling fee for US, UK and Australian sellers — but 0% is not free. You still pay payment processing of 3.3% + $0.45 per sale in the US, calculated on the item price plus buyer-paid shipping and any applicable tax. Boosted Listings adds an optional 12% on sales attributed to the boost. Sellers outside the US, UK and Australia may still pay a 10% selling fee.

Who pays for shipping on Depop vs Poshmark?

On Poshmark the buyer pays a flat $6.49 for a USPS Ground Advantage label up to 5 lb, and the seller pays nothing for postage under 5 lb. On Depop the seller sets the shipping and funds the label — you choose the carrier and the service, and the label cost comes out of your payout. Depop's payment processing is also calculated on the item price plus buyer-paid shipping, so shipping you charge the buyer raises the fee base.

Can I sell the same item on Depop and Poshmark at the same time?

Yes — cross-listing the same item on both is common. Price each listing for that platform's fees, and delete the other listing the moment one sells, because a double sale means cancelling one order and refunding a buyer. The fee table in this guide tells you which listing to price higher: the same jacket can be listed a few dollars higher on Poshmark and still bank the same amount.

Which is better for a beginner seller, Depop or Poshmark?

Depop is cheaper on fees and reaches a younger, trend-focused audience — best for streetwear, vintage and Y2K fashion. Poshmark is simpler: the buyer pays a flat $6.49 label, there is no shipping to configure, and its audience is broader and skews toward mainstream and premium brands, with bundles and live selling built in.
