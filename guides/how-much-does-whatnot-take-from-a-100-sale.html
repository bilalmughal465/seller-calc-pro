How Much Does Whatnot Take From a $100 Sale? A Worked Example for Sellers

Short answer: Whatnot takes $11.20 from a $100 sale for a US seller on the Standard tier — an 8% commission on the item price ($8.00) plus payment processing of 2.9% plus $0.30 ($3.20) — and you keep $88.80 before your item cost. There is no listing fee and no monthly subscription. The one catch is the fee base: the processing fee is charged on the item price plus any shipping the buyer pays and any applicable sales tax, so $9.21 of buyer-paid shipping raises the total fee to $11.47. The table below shows the fee at every common sale size, and the worked example shows the full profit maths on a $100 trading card.

How the 8% + 2.9% + $0.30 works — and the shipping trap

Whatnot charges sellers two fees on every sale: a commission on the item's final sale price and a payment-processing fee on the total order value. The commission is 8% for most categories on the Standard tier (4% for coins and money), and it applies only to the item price — buyer-paid shipping and buyer-paid tax are not in its base. The processing fee is 2.9% of the total order value plus a flat $0.30 per transaction, and the total order value is the item price plus shipping the buyer pays plus any applicable sales tax. Listing is free, so you pay only when something sells. See the Whatnot Seller Fees guide for the full fee structure in plain language.

The trap is that the shipping money goes to the carrier label, not to you — but Whatnot still takes 2.9% of it. A $100 item with $9.21 of buyer-paid shipping (a USPS flat-rate label for 1–5 lb) pays 2.9% of $109.21 plus $0.30 = $3.47 in processing, instead of $3.20. The total fee is $8.00 + $3.47 = $11.47. You keep $97.74 of the $109.21, but $9.21 of that is the shipping money for the label, so your real keep on the item is $88.53. Sales tax works the same way: the tax is not your money, but you still pay 2.9% on it. Offering free shipping shrinks the processing base to the item price alone — $11.20 on a $100 sale — but then the label cost comes out of your pocket. Which setup wins depends on your label cost and whether free shipping lets you charge a higher price, so run both through the Shipping Cost Comparison Calculator before you choose.

The worked $100 example: a trading card, bought for $40

You list a trading card for $100.00, a buyer buys it at full price with no shipping charged, and you sourced it for $40.00.

Amount
Sale price$100.00
Whatnot commission (8% x $100.00)$8.00
Payment processing (2.9% x $100.00 + $0.30)$3.20
Total Whatnot fee$11.20
You keep (before item cost)$88.80
Item cost$40.00
Your profit$48.80

Check the maths. The commission is 8% × $100.00 = $8.00. The processing fee is 2.9% × $100.00 = $2.90, plus $0.30 = $3.20. Together that is $11.20. Subtract from the sale price: $100.00 − $11.20 = $88.80. Subtract your $40.00 item cost: $88.80 − $40.00 = $48.80 profit, a 48.80% margin on the sale price. Now the same card with $9.21 of buyer-paid shipping: the buyer pays $109.21, the processing fee is 2.9% × $109.21 + $0.30 = $3.47, the total fee is $11.47, you keep $97.74, and after the $9.21 label and your $40.00 item cost your profit is $48.53 — $0.27 less than the no-shipping version, because the processing fee also bites the shipping.

Why old Whatnot fee advice is wrong

Whatnot changed its fee structure in September 2026, and outdated advice is still everywhere. Until 21 September 2026, every seller paid a flat 8% commission. From that date, Whatnot sets your commission rate from your total sales over the previous four weeks: 8.00% is the Standard rate for most categories (4% for coins and money), and higher-volume sellers pay lower rates in the next four-week period. In select categories — comics and anime, toys and hobbies, coins and money, trading card games, entertainment cards, sports singles, bags and accessories, and jewelry and watches — the commission is 0% on the part of an item's price above $1,500, for a limited time. The Premier Shop 10% commission discount ended on 31 October 2026, so from 1 November 2026 Premier Shop rates depend only on the sales tier. Payment processing is untouched: 2.9% and $0.30 apply at every tier. If you see a flat "Whatnot takes 8% plus 2.9%" without any mention of tiers, it is from before September 2026 — and if a calculator still adds a listing fee, it is wrong too, because Whatnot has never charged one.

What else can take a cut

The 8% plus 2.9% plus $0.30 is the whole standard fee, but three things can still reduce your payout. Buyer-paid shipping grows the processing base, as the section above shows — $11.47 instead of $11.20 on a $100 sale with $9.21 shipping. Sales tax is collected from the buyer and remitted by Whatnot, but the 2.9% still applies to it, so a taxed sale costs you a few cents more in fees. Your sales tier can move the commission either way: the Standard tier is 8%, but a high-volume seller pays less, so the $11.20 above is the starting rate, not the only one — check your rate in Seller Hub before you price a big drop. None of these is in the $11.20 above, so treat that figure as the fee, not the whole story. Compare platforms in the Seller Fees Comparison Calculator.

Whatnot vs the other marketplaces on $100

Whatnot's $11.20 sits in the middle of the $100 series — more than Depop's $3.75 and Mercari's $10.00, less than eBay's $14.00 and Poshmark's $20.00. The gap only closes if you sell at volume: a lower commission tier can bring Whatnot under eBay by more, while a low sale price pushes the $0.30 processing fee up as a share of the sale.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Whatnot take from a $100 sale?

Whatnot takes $11.20 from a $100 sale for a US seller on the Standard tier — an 8% commission on the item price ($8.00) plus payment processing of 2.9% plus $0.30 ($3.20) — and you keep $88.80 before your item cost. There is no listing fee and no monthly subscription. The $11.20 assumes no buyer-paid shipping: processing is charged on the item price plus any shipping the buyer pays and any applicable sales tax, so $9.21 of buyer-paid shipping raises the total fee to $11.47.

Does Whatnot charge a listing fee?

No. Whatnot charges no listing fee, no monthly subscription and no application fee. You pay only when something sells: the commission on the item price and the payment-processing fee on the order total. Creating, storing and managing listings costs nothing.

Does Whatnot charge fees on shipping?

The commission does not apply to shipping, but the payment-processing fee does. Processing is 2.9% plus $0.30 on the total order value — item price plus shipping the buyer pays plus any applicable sales tax. A $100 item with $9.21 of buyer-paid shipping pays 2.9% of $109.21 plus $0.30, or $3.47 in processing, instead of $3.20. The shipping money itself pays for your label, so your real keep on the item is $88.53.

What is Whatnot's fee on a $50 sale?

Whatnot takes $5.75 from a $50 sale on the Standard tier — an 8% commission ($4.00) plus 2.9% plus $0.30 processing ($1.75) — and you keep $44.25 before your item cost. On a $25 sale it takes $3.03 and you keep $21.97; on a $10 sale it takes $1.39 and you keep $8.61. All of these assume no buyer-paid shipping; shipping the buyer pays is added to the processing fee base.

Is Whatnot or eBay cheaper on a $100 sale?

Whatnot is cheaper on the fee line. On a $100 sale Whatnot takes $11.20 — 8% commission plus 2.9% plus $0.30 processing — and you keep $88.80, while eBay takes $14.00 in most categories and you keep $86.00, a $2.80 difference. Whatnot's rate is tiered by your sales over the previous four weeks, so a high-volume seller pays less than 8% commission, while eBay's final value fee also varies by category.

What is Whatnot's tiered commission?

Since 21 September 2026, Whatnot sets your commission rate from your total sales over the previous four weeks. The Standard tier is 8% for most categories and 4% for coins and money, and higher-volume sellers pay lower rates. In select categories the commission is 0% on the part of an item's price above $1,500, for a limited time. Payment processing of 2.9% plus $0.30 applies at every tier.
