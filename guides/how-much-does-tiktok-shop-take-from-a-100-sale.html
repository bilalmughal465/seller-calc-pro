How Much Does TikTok Shop Take From a $100 Sale? Referral Fee, Worked Examples and the 8% Question

Short answer: TikTok Shop takes $6.00 from a $100 sale at the published 6% referral fee, and you keep $94.00 before your item cost. There is no fixed per-order fee, and payment processing is included in the 6%. But many sellers report seeing 8% in their Seller Center since August 2026 — at 8% the fee is $8.00 and you keep $92.00. Run your own sale through the TikTok Shop Fee Calculator before you list.

Stand: November 2026. Sources: TikTok Shop Seller Academy referral-fee table (6% most categories, 5% fine jewelry and pre-owned), re-checked 26 November 2026; the 8% rate is reported by sellers and trade press since August 2026 and is not in TikTok's public rate table. Rates change — check your Seller Center before you price. No sales-tax or income-tax advice is given here.

The formula

Referral fee = (item price + shipping charged − sales tax) × referral rate

Referral rate = 6% in most categories, 5% for fine jewelry and pre-owned

Fixed fee per order = $0

One charge applies to every sale, and it is the only mandatory one. The referral fee is a percentage of the customer payment — the item price plus any shipping you charge the buyer, minus sales tax — and it already includes payment processing, so there is no separate processing charge on a domestic order. There is no listing fee and no fixed per-order fee, which is why TikTok Shop looks so cheap next to eBay and Etsy. Everything else — creator commissions, refund fees, fulfilment, ads — only applies in its own case, and each one is covered below.

Worked example 1: a $100 item at the published 6%

A $100 item in a standard category, free shipping, no sales tax, no creator commission, no ads.

Fee base. $100.00 — item price only, because nothing else was charged.

Referral fee. $100.00 × 6% = $6.00.

Total TikTok Shop fees. $6.00. You keep $94.00 before your item cost — a 6.0% take rate, the lowest headline rate of any major US marketplace in this guide series.

Worked example 2: the same sale at the reported 8%

The same $100 item, but your Seller Center shows the 8% rate that many sellers report seeing since August 2026.

Referral fee. $100.00 × 8% = $8.00.

Total TikTok Shop fees. $8.00. You keep $92.00 before your item cost. The difference from the published rate is $2.00 on every $100 of sales — $200 across 100 orders — so check which rate your shop actually pays before you set prices. See the TikTok Shop fees explained guide for the full fee stack.

Worked example 3: the same sale with $8 shipping charged

The same $100 item, but the buyer pays $8 for shipping.

Fee base. $100.00 + $8.00 shipping = $108.00 (sales tax is not in the base).

Referral fee at 6%. $108.00 × 6% = $6.48. You receive $108.00 − $6.48 = $101.52.

Referral fee at 8%. $108.00 × 8% = $8.64. You receive $108.00 − $8.64 = $99.36.

Worked example 4: the profit after the item cost

You buy an item for $40, sell it for $100 with free shipping, and pay $7 to ship it yourself.

At 6%. You keep $94.00 from the sale, less the $40 item cost and the $7 shipping: $94.00 − $40.00 − $7.00 = $47.00 profit.

At 8%. $92.00 − $40.00 − $7.00 = $45.00 profit.

With a 10% creator affiliate commission. The creator takes $10.00 on the $100 sale, so at 6% the profit is $94.00 − $40.00 − $7.00 − $10.00 = $37.00. The affiliate commission is optional — you set it — but a 10% commission costs more than the referral fee itself, so set it against the margin, not the sale price. What else can TikTok Shop charge on the same sale?

Creator affiliate commission. If you offer a commission to creators, it is a separate cost on top of the referral fee — 10% is $10.00 on a $100 sale, 15% is $15.00. It is the cost that most often turns a profitable TikTok Shop listing into a loss, because it is bigger than the referral fee itself.

Refund administration fee. When a customer refund is issued, TikTok Shop charges 20% of the original referral fee, capped at $5 per SKU — $1.20 on a $100 sale at 6% ($1.60 at 8%). Small per order, but it lands on top of the refunded sale, so a high return rate eats the margin twice.

Fulfilled by TikTok. FBT per-unit costs start at about $4.28 depending on order size and weight, with free storage limited to 60 days before storage fees kick in. It is optional — self-fulfilment avoids it entirely.

Ads and samples. TikTok Shop ads and creator seeding samples are separate costs again. A 15% creator commission plus ad spend is how a $100 order's total cost of selling reaches 20%, 30% or more before the item cost — the 6% headline is only the first line of the stack.

Five mistakes that eat the TikTok Shop margin

Pricing on 6% when your Seller Center shows 8%. The $2.00 gap on a $100 sale is $200 across 100 orders. Open Seller Center and read the rate on your own shop before you set prices.

Forgetting the affiliate commission. A 10% creator commission ($10.00) costs more than the referral fee ($6.00). Set the commission against the margin — see the affiliate commission calculator — not against the sale price.

Forgetting the fee is charged on shipping. A $100 item with $8 shipping is a $108 fee base, not $100 — $6.48 at 6%, not $6.00. Price the fee on the total the buyer pays.

Treating 6% as the total cost. Refund fees, FBT, ads and samples stack on top of the referral fee. The headline is the first line, not the whole cost — see the fees explained guide for the stack.

Pricing without the item cost. $94.00 kept is not $94.00 profit — subtract what you paid for the item and the shipping first, as in worked example 4. Use the TikTok Shop Profit Calculator to check the real margin before you list.

Method and limits

The formula is referral fee = (item price + shipping charged − sales tax) × referral rate, with no fixed fee per order — the same approach the SellerCalc TikTok Shop Fee Calculator uses. The rates are TikTok Shop's published referral-fee table as re-checked 26 November 2026: 6% in most categories, 5% for fine jewelry and pre-owned categories (3% on the pre-owned portion above $10,000), new-seller introductory rate reported at 3% for the first 30 days. The 8% rate is disputed: it is reported by sellers and trade press as appearing in Seller Center since August 2026, but it is not in TikTok's public rate table, so this guide shows both and tells you to use your own Seller Center rate. The refund administration fee is 20% of the original referral fee, capped at $5 per SKU. The marketplace figures in the comparison table are taken from SellerCalc's own $100-sale guides, each worked on the same no-shipping, no-tax basis. This guide covers the fee calculation only: it does not cover listing strategy, advertising, returns, or the tax you owe on the profit — that is a question for a tax professional, not this guide. Frequently asked questions

How much does TikTok Shop take from a $100 sale?

TikTok Shop takes $6.00 from a $100 sale at the published 6% referral fee, and you keep $94.00 before your item cost. There is no fixed per-order fee, and payment processing is included in the 6%. If your Seller Center shows the 8% rate many sellers report since August 2026, the fee is $8.00 and you keep $92.00.

What percentage does TikTok Shop take from a sale?

TikTok Shop takes a 6% referral fee in most categories — 5% for fine jewelry and pre-owned categories — with no fixed fee per order. The fee covers the commission and the payment processing, so there is no separate processing charge on a domestic order. The fee base is the customer payment (item price plus shipping charged) minus tax.

Is the TikTok Shop fee 6% or 8%?

TikTok's published rate table says 6% for most categories, but many sellers report seeing 8% in their Seller Center since August 2026, and trade press reports the same. The 8% rate is not in TikTok's public rate table, so it is disputed — not confirmed. Use the rate your own Seller Center shows, and price on that, not on the headline 6%.

Does TikTok Shop charge fees on shipping?

Yes. The referral fee is charged on the customer payment including any shipping you charge the buyer, minus tax. A $100 item with $8 of shipping charged has a $108 fee base, so the fee is $6.48 at 6% (or $8.64 at 8%), not $6.00. Free shipping is not free of fees either, because the higher item price that covers it is also fee-charged.

Do new TikTok Shop sellers pay a lower fee?

New sellers get a reduced introductory referral rate for a limited time after their first qualifying sale — reported as 3% for the first 30 days. At 3% a $100 sale costs $3.00 in fees and you keep $97.00. The exact introductory offer changes, so check your Seller Center for the rate on your shop.

What else does TikTok Shop charge besides the referral fee?

Creator affiliate commissions are the big one: if you offer a 10% commission, that is another $10.00 on a $100 sale, on top of the referral fee. A refund triggers a refund administration fee of 20% of the original referral fee, capped at $5 per SKU — $1.20 on a $100 sale at 6%. Fulfilled by TikTok storage and fulfilment, ads and samples are separate costs again.
