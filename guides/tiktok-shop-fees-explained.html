TikTok Shop Fees Explained: What a $25 Sale Actually Keeps

Short answer: On a $25 item with $5 buyer-paid shipping, TikTok Shop's 6% referral fee is $1.80, and the sale keeps $14.20 after a $5 shipping label, an $8 item cost and $1 packaging. Add a 15% creator commission ($3.75) and the same sale keeps $10.45. The 6% headline rate is real, but it is not the whole cost — shipping, creator commission and refund fees all come out of the same payout.

Stand: November 2026. Sources: TikTok Shop Seller Center fee pages as reported by current seller guides. Fee rules change, and some guides have reported an 8% US rate while TikTok's own fee page showed 6% for most categories — check your category's rate in Seller Center before you price. No sales-tax or income-tax advice is given here.

The fee rules, in plain language

ChargeUS rule used in this guide
Opening a shop / listingFree — no monthly fee, no listing fee
Referral fee6% for most categories (around 5% for some, such as jewelry) on item price plus buyer-paid shipping, before sales tax; payment processing is included
New-seller rateA temporary lower rate for a short window after a shop's first qualifying sale — some guides report 3% for 30 days, others a 2% discount for 90 days; treat it as temporary, not as your pricing rate
Creator (affiliate) commissionSet by the seller, typically 10% to 20% for open collaborations, on the item price; deducted in addition to the referral fee
Refund administration fee20% of the original referral fee, capped at $5 per item, on a refunded order
Not in the tablesAds, Fulfilled by TikTok storage and per-unit fees, shipping subsidies and co-funded voucher splits, and return shipping where the seller is responsible

Read the fee base, not just the rate. TikTok Shop charges its referral fee on the item price plus the shipping the buyer pays, so a sale with $5 shipping is fee'd on $30, not $25. And some guides list a separate payment-processing charge of around 2% — TikTok's own fee page treats the referral fee as covering platform fees apart from shipping and tax, so do not add a second processing fee on top unless your Seller Center statement shows one.

Worked walkthrough: one $25 sale, three versions

All versions are US sales. “You keep” is after TikTok Shop's fee, the shipping label you buy, your item cost and packaging — before ads, returns and income tax.

VersionReferral feeOther costsYou keep
No creator: $25 item, $5 shipping charged, $5 label, $8 item, $1 packaging$1.80$5 label + $8 item + $1 packaging$14.20
With a creator at 15%: same sale$1.80$3.75 creator commission, plus $5 label + $8 item + $1 packaging$10.45
New-seller 3% rate, no creator: same sale$0.90$5 label + $8 item + $1 packaging$15.10

Check the maths on the first row. The fee base is $25 + $5 = $30, so 6% x $30 = $1.80. Then $30.00 - $1.80 - $5.00 label - $8.00 item - $1.00 packaging = $14.20. The creator version subtracts 15% x $25 = $3.75 more, giving $10.45. The new-seller version uses 3% x $30 = $0.90, giving $15.10 — only $0.90 better, which is why a pricing plan built on the temporary rate breaks the day the window closes.

A second sale, at $60, to show the pattern

On a $60 item with $8 buyer-paid shipping, an $8 label, a $20 item cost, $1 packaging and a 15% creator commission: the referral fee is 6% x $68 = $4.08, the creator commission is 15% x $60 = $9.00, and you keep $68.00 - $4.08 - $8.00 - $20.00 - $1.00 - $9.00 = $25.92. The pattern is the same at every price: the referral fee scales with item plus shipping, the creator commission scales with the item price alone, and the fixed label and packaging costs weigh heaviest on cheap items. If the $25 sale is refunded, the refund administration fee is 20% x $1.80 = $0.36 — small on its own, but it comes on top of returning the item's money, and return shipping may be yours too if the return is your responsibility.

Method and limits

Figures use the published US rules as reported in November 2026: a 6% referral fee for most categories on item price plus buyer-paid shipping before sales tax, processing included; creator commission set by the seller (15% used in the examples); a refund administration fee of 20% of the original referral fee capped at $5 per item. Examples exclude sales tax, ads, Fulfilled by TikTok fees, shipping subsidies and co-funded voucher splits, and treat the shipping label you buy as equal to the shipping you charge. Frequently asked questions

What percentage does TikTok Shop take from a sale?

For most US categories, TikTok Shop takes a 6% referral fee on the item price plus the shipping the buyer pays, before sales tax, and that fee includes payment processing. Some categories are lower, around 5%, and new sellers may get a temporary lower rate. Your Seller Center rate for your category is the final word.

Does TikTok Shop charge a listing fee or a monthly fee?

No. Opening a shop and listing products is free for US sellers. TikTok Shop's charges come from orders: the referral fee on each completed sale, plus optional costs such as creator commission, ads, and a small fee on refunded orders.

Does TikTok Shop charge its fee on shipping?

Yes, on the shipping the buyer pays. The referral fee is charged on the item price plus buyer-paid shipping, before sales tax. If you offer free shipping, the fee is on the item price only, but you still pay the shipping label yourself.

What happens to TikTok Shop fees when an order is refunded?

A refunded order triggers a refund administration fee equal to 20% of the original referral fee, capped at $5 per item. On a $25 item with a $5 shipping charge, the referral fee is $1.80, so the refund admin fee is $0.36. Return shipping and other deductions depend on who is responsible for the return.

Is creator commission charged on top of the referral fee?

Yes. Creator (affiliate) commission is set by the seller, typically 10% to 20% for open collaborations, and it is deducted in addition to the referral fee. On a $25 item, a 15% creator commission is $3.75, which cuts the $14.20 kept on a no-creator sale down to $10.45 in the walkthrough above.
