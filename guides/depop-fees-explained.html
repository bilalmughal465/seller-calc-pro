Depop Fees Explained: What US and UK Sellers Actually Pay

Short answer: Depop charges US and UK sellers no selling fee — 0%. What you actually pay is payment processing: 3.3% + $0.45 per sale in the US, or 2.9% + £0.30 in the UK, calculated on the item price plus buyer-paid shipping and any applicable tax. A $50 US sale with no shipping and no Boost keeps $47.90 before your own costs.

Stand: November 2026. Sources: Depop Help Centre — Seller fees and charges. Fee rules change — check Depop's current fee page before you price.

How the fees changed: the old 10% is gone in the US and UK

Older guides still say "Depop takes 10%." That was the old selling fee, and it no longer applies to current listings in the main markets. Depop removed it market by market: for UK sellers from 20 March 2024, for US sellers from 15 July 2024, and for Australian sellers from 22 July 2026. Sellers outside the US, UK and Australia who sell in a currency other than GBP, USD or AUD may still pay a 10% selling fee, plus payment processing that varies by location.

Worked examples: the same sale, before and after Boost

All figures below are before your own costs, such as the item's cost and packaging.

Sale (US, no Boost)Processing feeYou keep
$20 item, no shipping$1.11$18.89
$50 item, no shipping$2.10$47.90
$100 item, no shipping$3.75$96.25

Check the maths on the $50 row: 3.3% x $50.00 = $1.65, plus $0.45 = $2.10, so $50.00 - $2.10 = $47.90. On the $20 row, the fixed $0.45 is a bigger slice: 3.3% x $20.00 = $0.66, plus $0.45 = $1.11, keeping $18.89. Cheap items always lose the most to the fixed part of the fee.

Shipping raises the fee base

Take a $40 US item with $5.00 of buyer-paid shipping. The processing base is $40.00 + $5.00 = $45.00, the fee is 3.3% x $45.00 + $0.45 = $1.49 + $0.45 = $1.94, and you keep $45.00 - $1.94 = $43.06 before any label cost you pay yourself. Shipping is never fee-free on Depop, because processing is calculated on item price plus shipping and tax — not on the item price alone.

Boosted Listings: the optional 12% that changes everything

Boosted Listings is optional promotion, and it is the one fee that can dwarf processing. In the US and UK it costs 12% of the item sale price on a sale attributed to the boost, charged on top of processing. Take a $60 US item sold through a Boost: processing is 3.3% x $60.00 + $0.45 = $1.98 + $0.45 = $2.43, the Boost fee is 12% x $60.00 = $7.20, total fees are $9.63, and you keep $50.37. That is close to 16% of the price in combined fees — still below Poshmark's 20% on a $60 sale, but a long way from the "0% fee" headline. Boost only items that are already priced and photographed well, and price for the 12% before you switch it on. Using a Depop shipping label can narrow the Boost fee base; arranging your own shipping can widen it, because the fee is then calculated on item price plus shipping.

Depop vs Poshmark, in one line

On a standard $50 US sale, Depop keeps $2.10 in processing while Poshmark takes $10.00 (20%), so Depop is far cheaper on fees alone. The comparison flips on audience: an item that sells on Poshmark and sits unsold on Depop earns more on Poshmark, whatever the fee table says. Match the item to where its buyers already shop first, then use the fee table to price it. See Poshmark vs Mercari Fees for the same comparison on those two platforms.

Method and limits

Figures use Depop's published US and UK fee rules as of November 2026: 0% selling fee in the US, UK and Australia; US processing 3.3% + $0.45 and UK processing 2.9% + £0.30 on item price plus buyer-paid shipping and applicable tax; Boosted Listings 12% of the item sale price in the US and UK on attributed sales. Buyer-paid amounts such as Depop's buyer Marketplace fee are not deducted from the seller's keep figures. Refunds reverse the fees tied to the sale. Fee rules change — always check Depop's current fee page before you price.

Frequently asked questions

Does Depop charge a selling fee?

Not for sellers in the US, UK or Australia: the selling fee is 0% there. Sellers in other markets selling in a currency other than GBP, USD or AUD may still pay a 10% selling fee.

What does Depop take from a $50 sale in the US?

On a $50 US sale with no buyer-paid shipping and no Boost, Depop takes $2.10 in payment processing (3.3% of $50 is $1.65, plus $0.45), so you keep $47.90 before your own costs.

Is Depop cheaper than Poshmark?

On seller fees alone, yes for a standard sale: Depop's US cost is 3.3% + $0.45 processing, while Poshmark charges 20% on sales of $15 and above. A 12% Boosted Listings fee on Depop narrows that gap, but Boosting is optional.

Does Depop charge a fee on shipping?

Payment processing is calculated on the item price plus buyer-paid shipping and any applicable tax, so buyer-paid shipping raises the processing fee base. Using a Depop shipping label can narrow the fee base for a Boosted sale.

What is Depop's Boosted Listings fee?

Boosted Listings is optional promotion. In the US and UK it costs 12% of the item sale price on a sale attributed to the boost, on top of payment processing. It applies only to boosted items that actually sell.
