Poshmark Shipping Checklist: 7 Checks Before You Print the Label

Short answer: Poshmark shipping is simple until it is not. The buyer pays a flat $6.49 for a USPS Ground Advantage label up to 5 lb, and you pay nothing for postage — until the parcel crosses 5 lb, the box does not match the service, the label belongs to another order, or the parcel sits unshipped for a week. Each of the 7 checks below comes with what it costs when you skip it, and the worked example at the end shows the full maths on a $25 jacket at 4 lb and at 6 lb.

Stand: November 2026. Sources: Poshmark Support shipping rates page and fee page ($6.49 flat buyer shipping, USPS Ground Advantage up to 5 lb, overweight costs the seller's responsibility; $2.95 under $15, 20% at $15 or more), Poshmark label upgrade tiers ($5.00 for 5.01–10 lb, $10.00 for 10.01–15 lb) and the 26 October 2026 overweight postage rule — re-checked 20 November 2026. Carrier rates and marketplace fee rules change — check the current Poshmark fee and shipping pages before you price or list. No sales-tax or income-tax advice is given here.

Check 1: Weigh the finished parcel first — the 5 lb line is where money leaves

What it costs: the standard label covers up to 5 lb. Over that you need an upgraded label: $5.00 more for 5.01 to 10 lb and $10.00 more for 10.01 to 15 lb, paid by the seller out of earnings. Since 26 October 2026, if you ship an overweight package on the standard label without upgrading, Poshmark deducts the extra postage from your earnings — the fee finds you either way. A $25 jacket packed at 6 lb costs a $5.00 upgrade; packed at 12 lb it costs $10.00. On a heavy bundle the upgrade can take half your payout.

The fix: pack the order completely — item, box or mailer, tissue, thank-you card — then weigh the finished parcel on a kitchen or postal scale before you print the label. Never use the item's weight from the listing: the packaging is what the scale sees. If you are close to 5 lb, a poly mailer instead of a box (1–2 oz against 6–8 oz for a cardboard box) can be the difference between $0 and $5.00. Run the numbers in the Poshmark Fee Calculator before you list heavy items, and see the Poshmark fees guide for the full fee table.

Check 2: Check the item before you pack it — the wrong item costs the whole sale

What it costs: a wrong item, a missed stain or a hole you never noticed turns into a return and a refund. On Poshmark a buyer who receives something that is not as described can return it, and the refund comes out of your payout — you lose the full $20.00 on a $25 sale, plus the time spent packing, messaging and re-listing. A cancelled sale costs you the whole payout, not just the shipping.

The fix: pull the item out and check it against the listing before it goes in the box: the right size, the right colour, no new stain, no hole, no broken zipper. Hold it under a bright light, check the seams and the cuffs, and give it a lint roll. Five minutes here is cheaper than a return, and it is the check sellers skip most when they are in a rush.

Check 3: Use Poshmark's label only — never your own postage

What it costs: Poshmark generates a prepaid label for every sale, and delivery is tracked through that label. Your own postage is not tied to the order, so the sale can sit showing as unshipped, tracking will not update in the app, and if the buyer opens a delivery dispute you have no Poshmark-tracked proof the parcel moved. The label is also tied to that one order: swapping labels between two orders sends each buyer the other's tracking, and both parcels look lost.

The fix: download the label from the order itself, print it, and attach it to that order's parcel only. If two orders sell the same day, pack and label them one at a time — never print both labels first and guess which parcel is which. A label swap is a small mistake with two angry buyers attached.

Check 4: Match the box to the service — a Priority box on a Ground Advantage label costs you

What it costs: Poshmark labels are now USPS Ground Advantage, not Priority Mail. Free USPS boxes are for Priority Mail and Priority Mail Express only. Put a Ground Advantage label on a Priority Mail box and USPS applies an automatic upcharge — you pay Priority Mail prices for a Ground Advantage service, and the difference comes out of your earnings. The same trap works the other way: a Flat Rate box only helps when the item is heavy for its size.

The fix: match the packaging to the service on the label. Plain boxes and poly mailers for Ground Advantage; USPS-branded boxes only with Priority Mail labels. Stock free Priority packaging only for the orders that actually ship Priority. Double-check the package type on the label screen before you click purchase — a wrong dropdown choice is one of the most common sources of label adjustments. See the cheapest way to ship a small package guide for the full box-and-service table.

Check 5: Ship within 3 business days — after 7 days the buyer can cancel

What it costs: Poshmark expects the parcel out within 3 business days of the sale. If it has not shipped after 7 days, the buyer can cancel the order for a full refund — and a cancelled sale costs you the whole payout, not just the shipping. A late parcel is the most common reason a sale turns into a cancellation, and it also earns the low ratings that slow your next sales.

The fix: ship the same day or the next day where you can, and keep shipping supplies on hand so a sale never waits for a box. Have items prepped at listing time — lint rolled, folded and stored where you can find them — and put your ship time in the listing ("orders ship in 1–2 business days") so buyers know what to expect. If a delay appears, message the buyer before the 7-day mark, not after it.

Check 6: Price the shipping discount before you offer it — it comes out of your pocket

What it costs: the discount comes straight out of your earnings. Standard shipping is $6.49, paid by the buyer. Offer $5.95 shipping and you pay $0.54. Offer free shipping and you pay the full $6.49. On a $12 top, $5.95 shipping leaves you $8.51 instead of $9.05 — and free shipping leaves you $2.56. On a $25 jacket packed at 6 lb, free shipping plus the $5.00 overweight upgrade leaves you $8.51 from a $25.00 sale. The discount buys conversion, but on cheap items it eats the profit first.

The fix: offer discounted shipping where the extra sales are worth more than the discount costs you — usually on items above $25, where $0.54 or even $6.49 is a small share of the payout, and never on items under $15, where the $2.95 flat fee already takes a fifth of the price. Price the discount into the listing before you offer it, and check the result in the Shipping Profit Calculator. See the Poshmark $100 sale guide for the full fee table.

Check 7: Bundle with the 5 lb line in mind — one fee, but one weight limit

What it costs: a bundle from your closet ships for a single $6.49 fee as long as the combined weight stays under 5 lb — three $15 tops in one parcel cost the buyer $6.49 total, or $2.16 per item. But the moment the bundle crosses 5 lb, the $5.00 upgrade (5.01–10 lb) lands on you, and a heavy bundle can cross 10 lb and cost $10.00. A bundle that looks like a bargain can quietly take $5.00–$10.00 out of your payout.

The fix: weigh the bundle packed before you accept it, and if it crosses 5 lb, message the buyer and suggest splitting it into two orders — yes, they pay shipping twice, but you avoid the upgrade fee, and most buyers understand when you explain it before shipping. Or upgrade the label before the parcel leaves, so the cost is planned, not deducted later. Use the Bundle Profit Calculator to check a bundle's payout before you accept the offer. See the Poshmark vs Mercari fees guide for where bundles work best.

The worked example: a $25 jacket, packed at 4 lb and at 6 lb

You sell a jacket for $25.00 on Poshmark. Poshmark takes 20% on sales of $15 or more, so the fee is $5.00 and you keep $20.00 before any shipping cost. The buyer pays $25.00 plus $6.49 shipping, or $31.49 in total.

Check the maths. At 4 lb: $25.00 − $5.00 = $20.00, and the buyer pays $25.00 + $6.49 = $31.49. At 6 lb: $25.00 − $5.00 − $5.00 = $15.00 — the upgrade alone takes a quarter of your payout. With $5.95 shipping at 6 lb: $25.00 − $5.00 − $5.00 − $0.54 = $14.46, and the buyer pays $25.00 + $5.95 = $30.95. With free shipping at 6 lb: $25.00 − $5.00 − $5.00 − $6.49 = $8.51 — the discount and the upgrade together take $11.49, nearly half the sale. The same jacket packed one pound lighter keeps the full $20.00. Two more numbers worth knowing: a $12 top keeps $12.00 − $2.95 = $9.05 (buyer pays $12.00 + $6.49 = $18.49 — shipping is 54% of the item price), and a bundle of three $15 tops keeps $45.00 − $9.00 = $36.00 while the buyer pays $45.00 + $6.49 = $51.49, or $2.16 of shipping per item. Method and limits

The figures are 2026 US rates: Poshmark's published US rates ($2.95 flat fee under $15, 20% at $15 or more, $6.49 flat buyer shipping for a USPS Ground Advantage label up to 5 lb) and the label upgrade tiers ($5.00 for 5.01–10 lb, $10.00 for 10.01–15 lb) as published by Poshmark and re-checked 20 November 2026. The 3-business-day ship expectation and the 7-day buyer cancellation rule are Poshmark's published seller rules. Many older guides still quote the old $7.67 Priority Mail rate — Poshmark switched standard labels to USPS Ground Advantage on 12 September 2025 and the buyer rate is now $6.49, so this guide uses the current rate, not the old one. The $0.54 and $6.49 discount costs are simple subtractions from the $6.49 standard rate ($6.49 − $5.95 = $0.54; free shipping = the full $6.49). All figures are US domestic, for sellers without any Poshmark promotion, with no signature confirmation. Poshmark runs shipping promotions that change what the buyer pays for a limited time — check the current fee and shipping pages before you price. This is general information, not tax advice.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Poshmark shipping cost?

The buyer pays a flat $6.49 for shipping on every Poshmark order, for a USPS Ground Advantage label covering packages up to 5 lb. The seller pays nothing for postage under 5 lb. Poshmark switched standard labels from Priority Mail to Ground Advantage on 12 September 2025, when the rate dropped to $6.49 — many older guides still quote the old $7.67 Priority Mail rate.

Who pays for shipping on Poshmark?

By default the buyer pays the flat $6.49 at checkout and Poshmark provides the prepaid label. The seller pays only in three cases: an overweight label upgrade, a shipping discount the seller chooses to offer, or extra postage deducted when a package ships overweight on a standard label. Poshmark's 20% commission (or $2.95 under $15) is charged on the item price only, never on the shipping.

What happens if my Poshmark package is over 5 lb?

The standard label covers up to 5 lb. Over that you need an upgraded label: $5.00 more for 5.01 to 10 lb and $10.00 more for 10.01 to 15 lb, paid by the seller out of earnings. A $25 jacket packed at 6 lb costs you a $5.00 upgrade, so you keep $15.00 instead of $20.00.

Can I use my own shipping label on Poshmark?

No — use the label Poshmark provides for that order. Poshmark generates a prepaid label for every sale and tracks delivery through it. Your own postage is not tied to the order, so the sale can show as unshipped, tracking will not update in the app, and a delivery dispute becomes much harder to win. Print Poshmark's label, and use the right box for the service on it.

How fast do I have to ship on Poshmark?

Poshmark expects the parcel out within 3 business days of the sale. If it has not shipped after 7 days, the buyer can cancel the order for a full refund. Ship the same day or the next day where you can: a late parcel is the most common reason a sale turns into a cancellation, and a cancelled sale costs you the whole payout, not just the shipping.

Does offering discounted shipping on Poshmark cost me money?

Yes — the difference comes straight out of your earnings. Standard shipping is $6.49, paid by the buyer. Offer $5.95 shipping and you pay $0.54; offer free shipping and you pay the full $6.49. On a $25 jacket packed at 6 lb, free shipping plus the $5.00 overweight upgrade leaves you $8.51 from a $25.00 sale. Discount shipping only where the extra sales are worth more than the discount costs you.
