eBay vs Etsy Fees: Which Keeps You More on the Same Sale?

Short answer: On the same US sale with no ads, Etsy keeps less. On a $60 item with $8 buyer-paid shipping, eBay takes $9.65 in fees in most categories and Etsy takes $6.91, so Etsy leaves you $53.09 against eBay's $50.35 — before your item cost and packaging. The catch is eligibility: Etsy only accepts handmade, vintage and craft-supply items, while eBay accepts almost anything.

Stand: November 2026. Sources: eBay selling fees page; Etsy Fees & Payments Policy. Fee rules change — check each platform's current fee page before you price. No sales-tax or income-tax advice is given here.

Read the base column, not just the headline rate. eBay's 13.6% looks like a single fee, but it is charged on a wider base than Etsy's 6.5%: eBay charges on shipping and tax, while Etsy's transaction fee skips US sales tax. Etsy's extra 3% + $0.25 processing fee then closes part of the gap, which is why the only honest comparison is the same item run through both fee stacks.

Worked examples: the same sale, both platforms

All examples are US sales with no ads, no Store subscription and no Offsite Ads. Sales tax is left out of these examples so the fee stacks stay comparable; in a real sale, eBay's fee base includes tax and Etsy's processing fee includes tax, which widens eBay's fee slightly. “You keep” is after platform fees and after the shipping label you buy, before your item cost and packaging.

Check the maths on the $60 row. eBay: 13.6% x $68.00 = $9.25, plus $0.40 = $9.65, so $68.00 - $9.65 - $8.00 label = $50.35. Etsy: $0.20 listing + 6.5% x $68.00 = $4.42 + (3% x $68.00 + $0.25 = $2.29) = $6.91, so $68.00 - $6.91 - $8.00 label = $53.09. The $2.74 gap is the whole story on a mid-priced item — and it grows with price, reaching $6.10 on the $150 sale, because both fee stacks are mostly percentages.

When eBay wins anyway

Fees are not the whole answer. First, eligibility: a general resale item, an electronic gadget or a new branded product cannot be listed on Etsy at all, so eBay is the only choice of the two. Second, audience: an item that sells on eBay and sits unsold on Etsy earns more on eBay, whatever the fee table says. Third, category: eBay's rate is not 13.6% everywhere — some categories are lower, and a Basic eBay Store lowers most categories to 12.7% for a monthly subscription, which narrows the gap on high-volume selling. Fourth, ads: one Etsy Offsite Ads order at 12% to 15% costs far more than the $2.74 base-fee gap, and one eBay Promoted Listings sale at a high ad rate does the same. Compare the fee, then compare the buyer pool, then decide.

Method and limits

Figures use the published US fee rules as of November 2026: eBay 13.6% final value fee in most categories plus a $0.40 per-order fee on orders over $10, charged on item price plus buyer-paid shipping and sales tax; Etsy $0.20 listing fee, 6.5% transaction fee on item price plus shipping and gift wrap (not US sales tax), and 3% + $0.25 payment processing on the total sale amount. Examples exclude sales tax, Store subscriptions, Promoted Listings and Offsite Ads, and treat the shipping label you buy as equal to the shipping you charge. Category rates, Store rates and ad rates change the result — always check each platform's current fee page before you price.

Frequently asked questions

Is eBay or Etsy cheaper in fees?

On the same US sale with no ads, Etsy is usually cheaper: on a $60 item with $8 buyer-paid shipping, Etsy fees are $6.91 against eBay's $9.65 in most categories. The gap narrows on very cheap items, where Etsy's fixed $0.20 and $0.25 charges weigh more.

Does eBay charge a fee on shipping?

Yes, in most categories eBay's final value fee is charged on the total amount of the sale, which includes the item price, shipping charged to the buyer and sales tax, plus a per-order fee of $0.40 on orders over $10.

Does Etsy charge a fee on shipping?

Yes. Etsy's 6.5% transaction fee is charged on the item price plus shipping and gift wrap you charge, and its 3% + $0.25 payment processing fee is charged on the total sale amount including shipping and any sales tax.

Can I sell the same item on both eBay and Etsy?

Only if the item fits Etsy's rules: Etsy allows handmade goods, vintage items (20 years or older) and craft supplies. General resale, electronics and most new branded goods fit eBay but not Etsy, so the cheaper fee is irrelevant if the item cannot be listed there.

Do eBay Promoted Listings and Etsy Offsite Ads change this comparison?

Yes, and they are not in the tables above. eBay Promoted Listings charges your chosen ad rate on the total sale amount, and Etsy Offsite Ads charges 12% or 15% of an attributed order, capped at $100. Either one can cost more than the base fee gap between the two platforms.
