How Much Does Etsy Take From a $100 Sale? A Comparison Table for Every Price

Short answer: Etsy takes $9.95 from a $100 sale for a US seller — a $0.20 listing fee, a 6.5% transaction fee ($6.50) and 3% + $0.25 payment processing ($3.25) — and you keep $90.05 before your own costs. That $100 figure assumes no shipping and no ads: Etsy charges its transaction fee on the shipping you collect too, and a sale that came through Offsite Ads costs $24.95 in total fees instead. The table below shows the fee at every common sale size, and the comparison sections show exactly what changes the $9.95.

Stand: November 2026. Sources: Etsy's published US Fees & Payments Policy as reported in October–November 2026 (listing fee $0.20, transaction fee 6.5% since 2022, US payment processing 3% + $0.25, Offsite Ads 15% under $10,000 a year and 12% over it, capped at $100 per order); seller guides cross-checked in November 2026 (Price Profit Tools, Feebound, CartMango, Printful, Size.ly). Etsy's fee rules change — check the current seller pages before you price or list. No sales-tax or income-tax advice is given here.

The formula, worked on $100

Etsy fee = $0.20 listing fee + 6.5% × (item price + shipping charged + gift wrap) + 3% × total order + $0.25. The transaction fee is charged on the item price plus shipping and gift wrap; the payment processing fee is charged on the total order amount, which includes the sales tax Etsy collects on your behalf.

Worked on $100 with no shipping or tax: listing $0.20, transaction 6.5% × $100.00 = $6.50, processing 3% × $100.00 + $0.25 = $3.25, total $9.95. You keep $100.00 − $9.95 = $90.05 before your own costs — the item cost, the label, packaging, and any ads, which are covered in the worked example below.

Offsite Ads: the fee that changes the answer most

Offsite Ads is Etsy's program that advertises your listings on Google, Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest and Bing on Etsy's own budget. You never pay unless it works: the fee is charged only when a shopper clicks one of these ads and buys the item within 30 days, and it is capped at $100 per order no matter how large the sale is.

At 12%: the ad fee is $12.00, so $21.95 in total and you keep $78.05. On the $50 row: 15% × $50.00 = $7.50, plus $5.20 = $12.70, and you keep $37.30; at 12% it is $6.00 + $5.20 = $11.20, and you keep $38.80. Shops under $10,000 in trailing 365-day sales can switch Offsite Ads off; above that threshold it is mandatory and the rate drops to 12%. If your margin on an item is 30%, a $50 sale through an ad leaves you about 25% of the price in fees — close to a loss. Etsy vs eBay at $100

On a plain $100 sale with no shipping or tax, Etsy is cheaper than eBay by $4.05. The comparison flips as soon as Offsite Ads apply.

eBay's $14.00 is 13.6% of $100.00 ($13.60) plus the $0.40 per-order fee, from the eBay $100 sale guide. Etsy has no category rates — a ring and a bookshelf pay the same 6.5% — while eBay's rate varies by category (clothing pays 15.3%). Etsy also has no fulfilment service, so the comparison leaves out postage, packaging and your own time, which Amazon FBA would handle for a fee. Worked example: buy for $35, sell for $100

You make a handmade item for $35.00 in materials and sell it for $100.00, with free shipping and no ads. Etsy takes $9.95, so your payout is $90.05. Subtract the $35.00 materials, the $8.00 shipping label you pay yourself, and $1.50 of packaging, and your profit is $45.55.

A $100 item with $6 shipping

Shipping changes the fee because it is part of the transaction fee's base. A $100.00 item with $6.00 shipping charged to the buyer is a $106.00 fee base: the transaction fee is 6.5% × $106.00 = $6.89, the payment processing is 3% × $106.00 + $0.25 = $3.43, and with the $0.20 listing fee Etsy takes $10.52 in total. The buyer paid $106.00, so you receive $95.48 before your own costs. If you had priced the same item at $100.00 with free shipping, the fee would be $9.95 — but you would pay the label yourself, so compare the label price first in the Shipping Cost Comparison Calculator before you choose. See the shipping cost guide for how to work out the label before you list.

Common mistakes when working out Etsy's fee

Quoting 6.5% as the whole fee. The transaction fee is 6.5%, but the listing fee and the payment processing add about 3.5 points more — $9.95 on $100, not $6.50. Forgetting the fee on shipping. The transaction fee is charged on the shipping you collect, so a $100 item with $6 shipping is a $106 fee base, not $100. Forgetting the listing fee renews. A sold listing renews automatically for another $0.20, so an active shop pays the listing fee on every sale, not just once. Finding out about Offsite Ads from an invoice. The 15% or 12% ad fee only applies to ad-attributed sales, but it is the single most common surprise charge — check your Shop Manager settings before you price. Pricing from the item price only. Materials, the label and packaging come out of the $90.05 you keep, not out of the $100 the buyer paid — the worked example above shows a $45.55 profit, not $90.05. See How to Price for Profit After Fees for how to build all of this into your price.

Method and limits

The standard rates are Etsy's published US rules as reported in October–November 2026: listing fee $0.20 per listing (renews every four months and after each sale); transaction fee 6.5% of the item price plus shipping and gift wrap, in place since 2022; US payment processing 3% plus $0.25 per order, charged on the total order amount including sales tax Etsy collects; Offsite Ads 15% for shops under $10,000 in trailing 365-day sales and 12% above it, charged only on ad-attributed sales and capped at $100 per order. The fee table excludes shipping, sales tax and ads, so on a shipped, taxed or ad-attributed sale every fee line is higher, as the sections above show. Non-US sellers see different payment processing rates, a regulatory operating fee in some countries, and a 2.5% currency conversion fee when the listing currency differs from the payout currency — those are not in the table. Etsy can change its fees at any time — always check Etsy's current seller pages before you price, promote or list.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Etsy take from a $100 sale?

Etsy takes $9.95 from a $100 sale for a US seller with no shipping and no ads: a $0.20 listing fee, a 6.5% transaction fee ($6.50) and 3% + $0.25 payment processing ($3.25). You keep $90.05 before your own costs. If the sale came through Offsite Ads, Etsy takes $24.95 (15% ad fee, shops under $10,000 a year) or $21.95 (12%, shops over $10,000 a year).

Does Etsy charge fees on shipping?

Yes. The 6.5% transaction fee is charged on the item price plus the shipping you charge the buyer plus any gift wrap — not just the item price. On a $100 item with $6.00 shipping, the transaction fee is 6.5% of $106.00, which is $6.89, and the payment processing is 3% of $106.00 plus $0.25, which is $3.43, so Etsy takes $10.52 in total and you receive $95.48 before your own costs.

What is Etsy's Offsite Ads fee?

Offsite Ads is Etsy's program that advertises your listings on Google, Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest and Bing. You pay only when a shopper clicks an ad and buys within 30 days: 15% of the order for shops under $10,000 in trailing 365-day sales (optional, can be switched off), or 12% for shops over $10,000 (mandatory). The fee is capped at $100 per order. On a $100 sale it adds $15.00 or $12.00 on top of the standard $9.95 in fees.

How much does Etsy take from a $10 sale?

Etsy takes $1.40 from a $10 sale for a US seller: a $0.20 listing fee, a 6.5% transaction fee ($0.65) and 3% + $0.25 payment processing ($0.55). You keep $8.60. The fixed fees ($0.20 plus $0.25) are a bigger share on small sales — 14% of a $10 sale against 9.95% of a $100 sale — which is why cheap items feel the fees most.

Is Etsy cheaper than eBay on a $100 sale?

Yes, on a plain $100 sale with no shipping or tax. Etsy takes $9.95 and you keep $90.05; eBay takes $14.00 in most categories (13.6% plus the $0.40 per-order fee) and you keep $86.00 — a difference of $4.05 in Etsy's favour. The comparison flips when Offsite Ads apply: a $100 Etsy sale through an ad costs $24.95 in total fees, against eBay's $14.00.

Does Etsy charge a monthly fee?

No, there is no monthly fee to sell on Etsy unless you add Etsy Plus at $10.00 a month, which is a bundle of listing credits and shop customisation, not a requirement. New shops pay a one-time setup fee, commonly $15 to $29 depending on country, when the shop is first opened. The listing, transaction and payment processing fees in this guide apply on every sale on top of that.
