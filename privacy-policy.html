Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026.

SellerCalc is a static website. There are no user accounts, no sign-up, and no login. We do not ask you to create an account to use a calculator.

What we do not collect

We do not run Google Analytics or any other analytics tracker on this site, and we do not collect names, email addresses or payment details through the calculators. That statement covers the calculators only — the contact form is separate (see Contact form below). Numbers you type into a calculator stay in your own browser for that calculation; they are not sent to us as a stored record.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, your name, email address and message are sent to us by email through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party service. We use that message only to reply to you.

Fonts

Pages load the Sora font from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google servers to fetch the font files. Google's privacy policy applies to that request.

Favorites and Recently used

Your Favorites and Recently used lists are stored only in your own browser (localStorage). They are never sent to us.

What a host may log

Like almost every website, the hosting provider may keep normal server logs for a short period, such as the page requested, the time, and a general network address, for security and reliability. We do not use those logs to identify individual visitors.

Links to other sites

This site links to platform help pages and other external pages. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their content or practices.
